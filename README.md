# Middle East

A NeoForge 1.21.1 mod that adds the Blandford's fox, a small desert fox that behaves exactly like a vanilla fox.

## Install

1. Download the `.jar` from the **Releases** section on the right (the "latest" release).
2. Put it in your `mods` folder together with **GeckoLib for NeoForge 1.21.1** (version 4.9 or newer).

## What it does

- Spawns in deserts in groups of 1–3, on sand, red sand or normal fox ground.
- All of its behaviour is the vanilla fox's: sleeps by day, stalks and pounces on chickens and rabbits, eats sweet berries, picks up and carries items, runs from players and wolves, and cubs bred by a player trust that player.
- Animations: walk, run, sleep from the Blockbench model, plus idle, sit, crouch and pounce.
- Spawn egg in the Spawn Eggs creative tab.

## Editing the model

The Blockbench file is `art/blandfords_fox.bbmodel`. After changing it, run `python3 tools/convert_model.py` to regenerate the model, animation and texture files.
